✨ Ongoing initiatives ✨

Real impact, real stories — our projects tackle hunger, education, environment, and animal welfare.

🐕‍🦺 🐈

🐾 Project Jeev

Animal protection & feeding initiative for stray dogs, cows, and abandoned animals. Rescue, rehabilitation, sterilisation drives, and emotional support for voiceless creatures.

✔️ 50+ animals fed daily | ✔️ adoption & shelter support
#IAFProjectJeev #CareForStrays
👩‍🎓📚

🕊️ Project Udaan

Empowering more than 900+ girls through education, skill workshops, and awareness campaigns. Promoting financial independence and self-confidence.

🎯 900+ girls empowered | 🧵 skill development
#ProjectUdaan #WomenEmpowerment
🌳🌏

🌿 Project Prakriti

Environmental protection through tree plantation, clean-up campaigns, and green awareness. Over 20,000 trees already planted! 💚

🌱 20,000+ saplings | 🌎 sustainable future drives
#ProjectPrakriti #GreenIndia
🍲📖

🤝 Project Seva & Bachpanshala

Food & clothes distribution for underprivileged families, plus quality education for children: school supplies, learning sessions, and nurturing young minds.

📦 Monthly ration support | 🎒 500+ children impacted
#ProjectSEVA #WinterRelief #EducationForAll
